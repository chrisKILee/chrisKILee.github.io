Everything you need to ship consistent AI products

From token extraction to component generation — Prism handles the full design-to-code pipeline.

Token Extraction

Connect your Figma file and Prism automatically extracts all design tokens — colors, typography, spacing, shadows — into a versioned token registry.

Component Generation

From tokens to production-ready React, Vue, or HTML components with zero drift. Every component is token-aware and instantly reflects design changes.

Design System Governance

Automated linting catches token violations before they reach production. Drift reports show exactly when and where your UI diverges from design specs.

Multi-Brand Theming

One component library, unlimited brand expressions. Switch themes at runtime or build time with CSS custom properties generated from your token sets.

AI Co-pilot

Describe a component in plain English and Prism's AI generates token-compliant code. Always respects your design system constraints — never hallucinate styles.

Analytics & Insights

Track token adoption, component usage, and drift rates across your entire product portfolio. Exportable reports for design system health reviews.
